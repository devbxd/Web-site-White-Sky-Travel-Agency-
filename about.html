A small agency that answers the phone

White Sky works from two offices, Kuwait City and Cheka in North Lebanon, with the same people following your trip from start to finish.

Who we are

Two offices, one point of contact

We handle flight tickets, hotel reservations, visa files and tailor-made packages for clients in Kuwait and in Lebanon, and for families travelling between the two.

Every request is handled by a person. We ask what the trip really needs, look for options that fit, and stay in touch by phone, WhatsApp or email until everything is booked.

No call centre and no scripts: the person who quotes your trip is the one you can call while you are away.
