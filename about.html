About White Sky

A travel agency built around direct, personal service — with a presence in both Kuwait and Lebanon.

Who We Are

Two offices, one point of contact

White Sky Travel Agency handles flight tickets, hotel reservations, visa assistance and custom travel packages from two offices — Kuwait City and Cheka, Lebanon — giving clients on both sides of the region a direct line to the same standard of service.

Every enquiry is handled personally: we ask what the trip actually needs, source options accordingly, and stay in touch by phone, WhatsApp or email until it's booked.

Our approach is deliberately straightforward — quality service, competitive prices, and journeys worth remembering, for every kind of traveller we work with.
